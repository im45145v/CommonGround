Aesop's Fables (selection, public domain; short retellings)

THE TORTOISE AND THE HARE
A hare mocked a tortoise for being slow, so the tortoise challenged him to a race. The hare ran far ahead,
lay down, and fell asleep. The tortoise kept plodding and crossed the line first.
Moral: Slow and steady wins the race.

THE NORTH WIND AND THE SUN
The North Wind and the Sun argued about who was stronger. They agreed to see who could make a traveller
remove his cloak. The harder the wind blew, the tighter the man held it. Then the Sun shone warmly,
and the traveller soon took it off.
Moral: Kindness is more effective than force.

THE ANT AND THE GRASSHOPPER
All summer a grasshopper sang while an ant stored food. When winter came, the grasshopper was hungry
and begged the ant for help.
Moral: Prepare today for the needs of tomorrow.
